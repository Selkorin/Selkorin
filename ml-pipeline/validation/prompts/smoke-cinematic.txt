## Quality Scoring Rubric
Score 0-10:
9-10: Perfect - strong smoke effect, dark cinematic mood, no artifacts
7-8: Good - clear smoke effect, correct mood, minor artifacts
5-6: Acceptable - smoke visible but weak, mood partially correct
3-4: Poor - barely visible smoke, wrong mood, some artifacts
0-2: Fail - no smoke effect, bright/wrong aesthetic, many artifacts
